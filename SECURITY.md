# Security Policy

Research records are untrusted data. This package parses local JSONL and does not fetch URLs.

- Do not open or execute downloaded artifacts merely because a record references them.
- Treat URLs, claims, publisher names, and attached files as untrusted.
- Avoid storing personal data, credentials, or non-public intelligence in public ledgers.
- Preserve original source material and record hashes separately when chain-of-custody matters.
- An evidence label is not a truth score or source-authenticity guarantee.

Report vulnerabilities with affected revision and a minimal reproduction. Never include credentials or private source data.
